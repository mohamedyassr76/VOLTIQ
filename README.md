VOLTIQ – Smart Meter Security Assessment Framework
VOLTIQ is a university research and development project focused on smart meter security assessment, energy systems, and cybersecurity. The project aims to develop a framework for evaluating the security of smart electricity meters and their communication systems, using the Iskraemeco MT174 as a reference device.

Our work includes researching the MT174 smart meter, studying its interfaces and communication mechanisms, and identifying potential cybersecurity risks related to access control, data integrity, and tampering. We are also developing a laboratory-based monitoring prototype using Python, MQTT, and Wireshark to collect events, detect suspicious activities, and generate security alerts supported by evidence.

In addition to the technical development, our team conducted research on ISO 50001, focusing on energy management systems and how its requirements and principles can support energy efficiency and improve energy management practices within El Sewedy University of Technology.

The project combines practical cybersecurity research, smart metering technologies, and energy management concepts to contribute to a more secure and efficient energy environment.
